Before you ask

Does Paws Off record what I type?

No. It has to see key presses to block them, but it never stores or sends them. While locked it keeps only the last few characters, just enough to spot your unlock word, and forgets them on unlock.

What if it gets stuck?

Click Unlock (your mouse always works), type your unlock word, or lock your computer. Paws Off lets go the moment your computer locks, sleeps or switches user.

How does paw detection know it's a cat?

People rarely hold four keys at once, or two keys for three seconds. Paws do it all the time. Paws Off counts held keys and locks when it sees that pattern. You can switch it off in one click.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes. The same app runs in your system tray on Windows 10 and 11, with Ctrl+Alt+L as the shortcut.

How many computers can I use it on?

All of yours. One purchase, any number of your Macs and PCs.

Does it need the internet?

No. Paws Off works completely offline. It only goes online to check for updates if you switch that on, and once a week to check that your licence key hasn't been cancelled. It never sends anything about you.
